# Code of conduct

## Purpose

This is a small, focused open-source project. This document exists to keep
participation respectful and technical so contributors can work together
productively.

## Expected behavior

- Keep discussion on the technical substance: algorithms, correctness,
  complexity, benchmarks, and documentation.
- Assume good faith and be patient with questions.
- Support benchmark and correctness claims with the details described in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately by email to
[lim@limsumin.com](mailto:lim@limsumin.com). Security vulnerabilities should
instead follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, lock, or remove contributions that violate this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or serious violations.
Enforcement decisions will be proportionate to the conduct involved.
